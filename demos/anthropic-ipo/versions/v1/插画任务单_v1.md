English:
> cinematic dark digital oil painting illustration, at least 70% black, amber orange (around #D97757) only in light beams, rim light and small highlights, deep red as minor accents, strong chiaroscuro, dramatic lighting, fine brushwork, low saturation, documentary editorial illustration, 16:9 landscape. No recognizable faces, no text, no logos.

- 16:9，**2560×1440 优先，最低 1920×1080**，PNG。
- 每张出 **2 个变体**，文件名 `I01_a.png`、`I01_b.png`，放 `demos/anthropic-ipo/素材/illus/`。
- 来源表每张登记：文件名、编号、生成工具和模型、完整提示词、生成时间。
- 底线：不画真人面孔（人物只画背影、剪影、手、脚）；不出现文字和公司标志（包括 Anthropic、OpenAI、英伟达的标志）；不加胶片颗粒。
