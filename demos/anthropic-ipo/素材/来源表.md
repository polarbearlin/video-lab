## 核对备忘（J19 原文用词）

- 现网稿与 Wayback 全文一致处：标题为 *Scoop — AI employees sound alarm: models too powerful to ever release*（2026-11-24）。
- “二十多位”：原文是 *more than two dozen sources familiar with the internal debates*，并未写死“二十多位员工”。
- “欺骗试图控制它的人类”：原文没有这句中文的逐字对应。最近的原文是研究员说 *they will in fact be able to trick us successfully*，以及 *deceptive "cover story" chains of thought to satisfy human monitors*。
- “应该立刻停止训练”：原文是 *Others aren't convinced and say companies should stop training new models entirely*（部分内部人士的主张，不是公司决定）。
- Anthropic 发言人回应已在关键段截图中：*Keeping a model internal doesn't make it safe. Our responsibility starts in training and covers how we use models ourselves, not only what we release. ... The hardest safety work is still ahead, and we've been clear that we may slow down or hold back even when it costs us.*
